Silpa — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Silpa ("the app") is a drawing and painting app published by [DEVELOPER NAME]. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Your artwork and images stay on your device

Drawings, projects, imported photos and everything in the Image Bank are stored only on your device. Image Bank sorting (categories, face-part cut-outs, colors, similarity) runs entirely on your device using on-device machine-learning models. We do not upload, collect or see your images or artwork. If you use Android's backup, your device may back up app data to your own Google account under Google's terms; we have no access to it.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a small banner ad on the Home and Image Bank screens, provided by Google AdMob. Before ads are requested, you are asked for consent where required by law (Google's User Messaging Platform). Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and information about ad interactions and app performance, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings. You can change your consent choice at any time from the app.

No ads are shown to Silpa Pro users.

Purchases

Silpa Pro is purchased through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google; we receive only confirmation that the purchase was made, so the app can remove ads. We never receive your payment details.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in is required, and we do not collect your name, email or contacts.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking beyond what is described above for advertising.

Children

Silpa is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

All app data is on your device. Uninstalling Silpa, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. Advertising data held by Google can be managed in your Google account and device settings.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes and revise the effective date above.
